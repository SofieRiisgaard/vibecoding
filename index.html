About me

Flour on my hands, salt in my hair.

I'm Sofie — a home baker with a soft spot for pretty desserts and a serious love for the outdoors. My kitchen is usually covered in flour, and my camera roll is mostly cakes and coastlines.

I bake because it makes people happy. A loaf of sourdough, a plate of cupcakes, a cake for someone's birthday — small things that turn an ordinary day into a little celebration.

When I'm not baking, you'll find me walking by the water, collecting shells, and taking photos of whatever catches the light. This site is where those two worlds meet.
